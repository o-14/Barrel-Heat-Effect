# Authored Card / Render Validation - 2026-08-06

Build: `dist\GunHeatHaze-0.4.0-authored-card.zip`

## Run this 2-minute check first

Your install has **both ENB and ReShade hooked**: `d3d11.dll` + `enbseries` +
`enbseries.ini`, and `dxgi.dll` + `reshade-shaders`. ENB replaces large parts of the
Fallout 4 renderer, and the mod's effect depends entirely on the game's refraction pass.

Before judging this build, confirm the pass works at all:

1. **Spin up a vanilla Minigun** and look at the barrel - that is the exact effect this
   mod is trying to reproduce.
2. Or fire a **Flamer** and look for heat distortion around the jet.
3. Or visit the **Glowing Sea** / **Saugus Ironworks** and look for the mirage shimmer.

If none of those distort the background, the ENB/ReShade stack is eating the refraction
pass and no mod-side work can fix it - that becomes the thing to solve first. A quick
A/B is to rename `d3d11.dll` (ENB) to `d3d11.dll.off`, launch, and re-check.

This matters because 0.3.4 showed nothing even at `fRefractionMaxStrength=0.60`, and
"the card is wrong" and "refraction does not render here" look identical from in-game.

## The authored asset

`GunHeatHaze\GunHeatBarrelRefractionCard.nif`, generated by
`tools\make_barrel_refraction_card.py` from `Effects\SaugusSmokeMirage.nif`.

Saugus was chosen over building from raw bytes because it *is* the audit recipe already,
and it is proven to render in vanilla as an ordinary world object:

```text
NiNode "GunHeatBarrelHaze"
├── NiBillboardNode "Cylinder026"     scale 0.25   <- already camera-facing
│   └── BSTriShape "Cylinder026:0"    91 verts, radius 120.24 -> 30.1 units
│       └── BSLightingShaderProperty
│           └── BSShaderTextureSet    SmokeVapor01Tile_n.dds in both slots
└── NULL                              (EditorMarker detached)
```

```text
flags1 = 0x80408201  Specular | Cast_Shadows | Refraction | Own_Emit | ZBuffer_Test
flags2 = 0x00000030  Double_Sided | Vertex_Colors        (ZBuffer_Write OFF)
Refraction Strength  0.25
```

It carries **none** of what made the old asset unusable: no `BSBehaviorGraphExtraData`,
no `BSConnectPoint::Parents`, no `BSValueNode` add-on nodes, no collision, no
`NiControllerManager`. There is no flame geometry anywhere in the file, so no amount of
engine machinery can produce fire.

Two differences from the old flamer card worth noting, both deliberate:

- **No `Fire_Refraction`.** The mirages, which render as plain world objects, do not set
  it. The flamer and minigun cards do - and both of those live inside weapon/effect rigs,
  not standalone. The mirage flag set is the one proven to work in our situation.
- **`Double_Sided` on, `ZBuffer_Write` off.** The old card was single-sided with depth
  write on, which is a plausible reason it was never visible regardless of strength.

### Animation

Saugus ships three chained controllers. Only the V Offset scroll is kept:

- **U Offset** animated a curve that is constant zero - a no-op.
- **Refraction Strength** oscillated 0.021-0.055 on its own, which would fight the DLL
  writing `refractionPower` every frame.

The surviving V Offset controller is a plain `NiTimeController` that ticks automatically
- nothing has to activate a named sequence. Its frequency is raised from `1.0` to
`19.996`, taking one full tile scroll from vanilla's 33.3 s down to **1.667 s, the
Minigun's exact cadence**.

## Runtime change

`bDriveRefractionStrength=0`.

With driving off, the card renders at the baked `0.25` for the whole puff lifetime. That
makes this a clean single question - *does refraction render at all here?* - instead of
a question tangled up with the fade curve. Set it back to `1` once refraction is
confirmed.

## Test

1. Install. The mesh is now `GunHeatBarrelRefractionCard.nif`; the old
   `GunHeatRefractionOnlyStrong.nif` is no longer referenced by the ESP.
2. Fire a burst at a well-lit, detailed wall.

- **No fire, ever.** There is no flame geometry in this asset.
- **Look for a circular patch of distortion** near the muzzle, roughly 30 units across,
  scrolling upward about once every 1.7 s.
- If it renders but is the wrong size, that is `--scale` on the generator (currently
  0.25). If it renders but is too strong or weak, that is `--refraction` (currently
  0.25, vanilla range 0.03-0.20).

## Then the delivery rewrite

I have deliberately **not** rewritten the delivery in this build. The rewrite replaces
the projectile carrier with an engine-owned art object attached to the weapon's
`fireNode` via `TESObjectREFR::ApplyArtObject` - it is a substantial change to the DLL,
the ESP, and the Papyrus layer, and none of it is verifiable while we still cannot tell
whether refraction renders on this machine at all.

If this build shows shimmer, the rewrite is unblocked and is the next thing I build. If
it shows nothing *and* the vanilla Minigun also shows nothing, the render stack is the
problem and the rewrite would have been invisible too.

The carrier's known defects are unchanged here: the effect stays where it spawned rather
than following the weapon, and puffs pop as they are born and die. That is exactly what
the rewrite fixes.
